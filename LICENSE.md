## Sources that no license can cover — conduct rule instead

Several sources (Modern C — CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; BARR-C:2018 — proprietary,
no-redistribution; comp.lang.c FAQ — personal-use only; SEI CERT C — © CMU)
prohibit derivatives outright. **No license choice makes copying them legal.**
This skill therefore never reproduces or adapts their text: their ideas are
restated in original words and cited with full URLs. See
[references/SOURCES.md](references/SOURCES.md) for per-source terms.
